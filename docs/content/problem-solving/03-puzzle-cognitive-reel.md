Floor-level perspective in a warm Bangladeshi home, focusing on Zayan, a 7-year-old Bangladeshi boy in a plain cotton t-shirt, warm brown skin, short slightly-messy black hair. His hands are carefully trying to fit a wooden puzzle piece together — framed on hands and torso, not direct face close-up, tongue slightly out in concentration. Seated nearby is Rahim, an elderly Bangladeshi man in his mid-60s in a simple white cotton panjabi and lungi, deep warm brown skin, grey-white hair, holding a tea glass and watching quietly, patient and unhurried without intervening.
