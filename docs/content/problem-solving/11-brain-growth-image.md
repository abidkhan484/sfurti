## Nano Banana Image Prompt

### How to use this prompt:
1. Open Nano Banana (app.nanabanana.ai or your interface)
2. Select "Text to Image" or "Generate Image"
3. Set dimensions: **1080×1350px** (portrait, more reach)
4. Paste the prompt below exactly
5. If result feels off, regenerate 2–3 times before modifying the prompt
6. Download highest resolution available

### Prompt:
A split-screen infographic style lifestyle image. Left side: a young Bangladeshi child (around 4-5 years old) sitting quietly and staring passively, a soft subtle screen glow illuminating their face, expressing a glazed, inactive look. Right side: the same child actively engaged, hands building something physical with wooden blocks or simple clay on the floor, bright and engaged expression, eyes focused on the hands. Warm golden hour window light, natural and inviting atmosphere. Earthy color palette, ochre, warm white, natural wood tones. Photorealistic, soft shadows, warm lifestyle photography, high detail. Empty space at the top and bottom third for text overlays.
