## Nano Banana Image Prompt

### How to use this prompt:
1. Open Nano Banana (app.nanabanana.ai or your interface)
2. Select "Text to Image" or "Generate Image"
3. Set dimensions: **1080×1080px** (square, Facebook feed)
4. Paste the prompt below exactly
5. If result feels off, regenerate 2–3 times before modifying the prompt
6. Download highest resolution available

### Prompt:
```
A minimalist, warm conceptual image of a small, healthy green sapling growing out of a stack of beautiful natural wooden building blocks. The setting is bathed in glowing golden hour light coming from the side. The color palette consists of earthy tones, soft warm terracotta, ochre, and warm white. Clean composition, rule of thirds, photorealistic, high detail. Ensure the right half of the image is mostly negative space with a soft warm wall background for text overlay. Hopeful, organic, natural aesthetic.
```
