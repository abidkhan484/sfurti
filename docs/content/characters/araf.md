## Veo3 Camera & Production Style

Apply these to EVERY scene featuring Araf:

- **Shot type:** Medium-close to close-up (waist to head for talking, full body when showing action)
- **Camera movement:** Slight natural sway — handheld documentary feel, not tripod-locked
- **Lighting:** Natural warm light — golden hour sunlight through window, or soft interior amber lamp. Never harsh studio light.
- **Color grade:** Warm, slightly desaturated, honest documentary feel. Think: a thoughtful parent's home video, not an ad.
- **Audio environment note:** Quiet ambient sound — ceiling fan hum, birds outside, light room tone. No music unless specified.
- **Pacing:** Slow, deliberate. Araf is never rushed. His energy is warm, not anxious.

---

## Nano Banana Image Style

Apply these to EVERY image featuring Araf or the brand:

- **Photorealism level:** Photorealistic, warm-toned lifestyle photography aesthetic
- **Composition:** Rule of thirds. Text space always reserved (top or bottom third for Bangla headline overlay)
- **Lighting:** Soft natural window light or golden hour. Warm temperature (5000K–6500K feel).
- **Color palette:** Earthy, warm — ochre, warm white, soft terracotta, natural wood tones
- **Avoid:** Blue-tinted light, high-contrast commercial look, stock-photo stiffness

---

## Hard Rules (apply to every piece of content)

1. Never shame parents for screen use. Frame everything as building something positive.
2. Never use fear-mongering language about child development.
3. Never make unverified developmental claims. Use real research if citing science.
4. Never name or show a specific product. "Cognitive toys" as a category is acceptable.
5. Never position any competitor as bad. Only position Sfurti's approach as good.
6. Always end with ভাবো, খেলো, বাড়ো or a clear ideological hook — never a hard sell.
