# Sara — Character Sheet
*Araf and Sonia's daughter. Elder sibling of Zayan. Granddaughter of Rahim and Fatema.*

---

## Identity

| Property | Detail |
|---|---|
| Name | Sara |
| Role | The older child — thoughtful, deliberate, quietly competitive with herself |
| Gender | Female |
| Age | 10 years old |
| Nationality | Bangladeshi |
| Relationship | Daughter of Araf and Sonia, elder sister of Zayan (7), granddaughter of Rahim and Fatema |

---

## Physical Appearance

| Property | Detail |
|---|---|
| Skin tone | Warm brown, a shade lighter than her brother |
| Hair | Long black hair, usually in two neat plaits or a high ponytail. Escaping wisps at the hairline. |
| Face | Longer than Zayan's, thoughtful eyes — the kind that notice things. Dark brown, observant. A slight furrow between brows when thinking. |
| Build | Slender, a little tall for her age — poised without being stiff |
| Hands | Deliberate. She traces, tests, measures with one finger before committing. Her hands move slowly compared to Zayan's. |

---

## Personality (show through body language, never through text)

- **Deliberate before she acts** — she studies the problem first. Longer pause before touching anything.
- **Dislikes being wrong** — not fragile about it, but she thinks before moving because she wants to be right
- **Quiet pride** — when she succeeds, it's a small smile and a stillness, not a jump. She sits with it for a moment before showing anyone.
- **Gently protective of Zayan** — sometimes helps him, sometimes competes with him, always aware of him
- **Observant of adults** — she notices when Araf or Sonia is watching and adjusts slightly. Not performatively — just aware.

## Key Expressions to Use in Veo3 Prompts

| Moment | What her face/body does |
|---|---|
| Concentrating | Sitting very still, one hand near chin, eyes moving across the problem |
| Testing something | One finger traces or touches tentatively before the full hand commits |
| Just succeeded | Small slow smile, a moment of stillness — then she might glance at Sonia or Araf |
| Helping Zayan | Leans toward him slightly, points without touching his work — she shows, doesn't do |
| Frustrated | Goes quiet. Moves the object slightly away. Takes a breath. Tries from a different angle. |

## Scene Assignment Logic

Use Sara in:
- Spatial reasoning / puzzle scenes requiring more deliberate thinking (ages 8–12 target)
- Scenes that address critical thinking, creativity, and EQ
- **Piece 38 (gender inclusivity Q&A)** — Sara is the visual anchor for this one
- Scenes where an older child models independent work for Zayan to observe
- Scenes with Sonia — mother-daughter closeness
- Scenes with Fatema (grandmother) — generational warmth
- Any "before/after" narrative: Sara struggling → Sara solving → quiet pride

**Camera note:** Avoid clear identifying close-ups of Sara's face. Frame on hands, partial profile, or over-shoulder. Protect child identity.

---

## Veo3 Prompt Notes

When writing Sara into a Veo3 prompt:
- Reference: "a 10-year-old Bangladeshi girl in a simple cotton dress or shalwar kameez, warm brown skin, long black hair in plaits, thoughtful dark eyes, deliberate and focused expression"
- Emphasize stillness and deliberateness — contrast with Zayan's energy
- Her hands: "tracing," "testing gently," "holding still," "measuring with one finger"
- Frame: over-shoulder, hands in foreground, or slight distance — never straight portrait close-up
