# Fatema — Character Sheet
*Araf's mother. Grandmother (Dadi) of Zayan and Sara. Wife of Rahim.*

---

## Identity

| Property | Detail |
|---|---|
| Name | Fatema |
| Role | Grandmother / environment creator / sanctuary |
| Gender | Female |
| Apparent age | Early 60s |
| Nationality | Bangladeshi |
| Relationship | Mother of Araf, wife of Rahim, grandmother (Dadi) of Zayan and Sara |

---

## Physical Appearance

| Property | Detail |
|---|---|
| Skin tone | Warm medium brown, soft with age — the skin of someone who has spent time in kitchens and sunlight |
| Hair | White-grey, always covered or tied back — often under a thin cotton orna or light scarf, worn loosely |
| Face | Rounded, full. Deep laugh lines. Eyes that crinkle when she smiles — and she smiles often. A mole or beauty mark near the jaw (distinctive detail for Veo3 consistency). |
| Build | Medium, comfortable — the fullness of a woman who has cooked for others for decades. Not frail. Solid and warm. |
| Hands | The most expressive part of her. Always moving — kneading, smoothing, handing, folding. Slightly work-roughened. These hands have made ten thousand meals. |

---

## Personality (show through body language, never through text)

- **Perpetually doing something** — she is never idle. Even when she sits, her hands are threading a needle or smoothing her orna.
- **Creates the environment** — Fatema is the reason the home feels like a home. She puts the mat on the floor, lays out the materials, adjusts the light. She doesn't direct the children's play; she makes the stage.
- **Feeder** — she appears with snacks at the exact right moment. A plate of muri, a glass of water. This is her language of care.
- **Storyteller** — in longer scenes, she is the one the children turn to when Araf isn't available. She tells stories from her hands as much as from her mouth.
- **Sanctuary** — children seek her physically. Zayan leans against her. Sara sometimes sits in her lap. Her presence lowers the temperature of any scene.

## Clothing

| Scene type | Clothing |
|---|---|
| Home / kitchen | Simple cotton saree (white with thin coloured border, or soft pastel — pale green, light blue). Worn casually, orna tucked at waist while working. |
| Prayer time | White saree, orna over head |
| With grandchildren | Same home saree — she doesn't change for the children, she's always dressed the same |
| NEVER | Synthetic fabric, heavy embroidery, jewelry beyond simple plain gold studs, anything that looks dressed-up |

---

## Environments

| Scene type | What Fatema is typically doing |
|---|---|
| Kitchen | Rolling roti, adjusting the stove, sorting lentils, cutting vegetables. Constant, practiced motion. |
| Living room / play area | Setting up the children's space — smoothing a mat, placing objects within reach, then stepping back |
| Courtyard | Hanging laundry, sitting with a colander of vegetables in her lap |
| With grandchildren | Zayan might be leaning against her side while she works. Sara sits near her doing her own thing. They are orbiting her. |

---

## Key Expressions to Use in Veo3 Prompts

| Moment | What her face/body does |
|---|---|
| Watching Zayan concentrate | Pauses what she is doing briefly. A quiet, full smile. Then goes back to work without disturbing him. |
| Handing a child something | Comes from the side, places it gently, touches the child's head once and moves away |
| Sara succeeding at something | Eyes crinkle before her mouth smiles. She says something — even if we can't hear it, it's brief and warm. |
| In background | Always doing something, but her body is angled toward where the children are |
| Storytelling | Hands slow down. Her face opens. The children lean closer. |

---

## Scene Assignment Logic

Use Fatema in:
- Storytelling videos (Pieces 46–48) — the domestic backdrop, the environment-setter
- Any scene set in the kitchen or family home where the ambiance matters
- Breakthrough/joy scenes — her reaction to a child succeeding is pure and undramatic
- Boredom scene (Piece 6) — she is nearby doing her work while Zayan mopes. She does not fix his boredom. She just continues.
- Any scene that needs to show "the home that enables this" — she IS that home
- Scenes with Sara — the grandmother-granddaughter thread has quiet warmth

Do NOT use Fatema as an explainer or narrator. She communicates through action and presence.

---

## Veo3 Prompt Notes

When writing Fatema into a Veo3 prompt:
- Reference: "a Bangladeshi woman in her early 60s in a simple cotton saree with a thin border, warm medium brown skin, white-grey hair covered loosely with a thin orna, rounded face with deep laugh lines, warm crinkled eyes, hands always in motion"
- Her hands must be doing something specific in every prompt: "rolling dough," "sorting lentils," "folding a cloth," "placing a glass of water"
- Camera: medium or wide — she is a figure in the home, not a subject of scrutiny
- She should almost never look directly at camera. She looks at the children, at her work, at the room.
