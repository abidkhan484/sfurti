# Sonia — Character Sheet
*Araf's wife. Mother of Zayan and Sara. Daughter-in-law to Rahim and Fatema.*

---

## Identity

| Property | Detail |
|---|---|
| Name | Sonia |
| Role | Mother / homemaker / quiet pillar of the household |
| Gender | Female |
| Apparent age | Late 20s (27–29) |
| Nationality | Bangladeshi |
| Relationship | Wife of Araf, mother of Zayan (7) and Sara (10) |

---

## Physical Appearance

| Property | Detail |
|---|---|
| Skin tone | Warm brown, smooth, with a natural glow — not fair, not dark, distinctly Bangladeshi |
| Hair | Long black hair, typically in a simple loose bun or a low plait. A few strands fall naturally around her face. Never styled elaborately. |
| Face | Soft facial features, gentle eyes (dark brown), naturally arched brows, a quiet smile that reaches her eyes before it reaches her lips |
| Build | Slender to average — not frail. She moves with quiet ease. |
| Hands | Slender, always doing something — adjusting a child's collar, handing something, smoothing a surface |

---

## Personality (show through body language, never through text)

- **Nurturing and quietly strong** — she doesn't lead loudly, she leads by presence
- **Observant before she speaks** — she watches what's happening before she says anything
- **Amused and patient** — small smiles at things others miss; rarely frustrated visibly
- **Emotionally present** — when she's in a scene, the child in the scene feels safe

---

## Clothing

| Scene type | Clothing |
|---|---|
| Home / everyday | Simple printed cotton shalwar kameez (soft pastels, light florals — nothing flashy). Orna (dupatta) worn loosely over shoulders, not pinned stiffly. |
| Kitchen / activity | Same, but orna tucked in at waist while working |
| Modest outdoor / market | Plain cotton salwar kameez, no prints — muted tones (soft sage, dusty rose, cream) |
| NEVER | Synthetic fabric, heavy jewelry, heavy makeup, Western clothing |

---

## Environments

| Scene type | What Sonia is doing |
|---|---|
| Kitchen | Rolling roti, arranging food on plates, washing produce — hands always busy |
| Living room | Folding laundry while watching the children play nearby. Or sitting on the floor with Sara doing something quiet. |
| Child's play area | Sitting nearby, not hovering — available if needed but letting the child lead |
| Doorway / threshold | Often appears in the background — the quiet anchor of the scene |

---

## Scene Assignment Logic

Use Sonia in:
- Any scene where a child is doing something and a parent is nearby observing without interfering
- Kitchen/home ambiance scenes (background warmth)
- Sara-forward scenes (mother-daughter resonance)
- Storytelling videos where the home environment is central
- Scenes that show the "family creates the environment" idea — she sets up the table, lays out the materials, then steps away

Do NOT use Sonia as a front-facing explainer or narrator. She is presence, not commentary.

---

## Veo3 Prompt Notes

When writing Sonia into a Veo3 prompt:
- Reference: "a Bangladeshi woman in her late 20s in a simple cotton shalwar kameez, warm brown skin, black hair in a loose bun, gentle expression"
- She should rarely look directly at camera. She looks at the children, at what she is doing, or into the middle distance with a quiet expression.
- Her energy: unhurried, warm, slightly tired but content — like a real mother, not an ad mother
