# Rahim — Character Sheet
*Araf's father. Grandfather (Dada) of Zayan and Sara. Husband of Fatema.*

---

## Identity

| Property | Detail |
|---|---|
| Name | Rahim |
| Role | Grandfather / elder wisdom / patient presence |
| Gender | Male |
| Apparent age | 65 years old |
| Nationality | Bangladeshi |
| Relationship | Father of Araf, husband of Fatema, grandfather (Dada) of Zayan and Sara |

---

## Physical Appearance

| Property | Detail |
|---|---|
| Skin tone | Deep warm brown, weathered with dignity — the texture of lived experience |
| Hair | Grey-white, short, well-kept. Sometimes wears a plain white cotton kufi (prayer cap), especially in morning or evening scenes. |
| Face | Deep laugh lines around eyes and mouth. The lines of a man who has smiled a great deal. Gentle eyes behind simple metal-framed reading glasses (worn low on nose, often held in hand). |
| Build | Slightly bent posture of natural aging — not frail, not weak. Dignified. The kind of posture that says 'I have sat with many things.' |
| Hands | Large, deliberate, slightly calloused. The hands of someone who has worked. When he gestures, it is slow and precise. |
| Beard | Short grey-white stubble or a short trimmed beard — natural, not groomed to perfection |

---

## Personality (show through body language, never through text)

- **Patient and unhurried** — he does not rush. He watches before speaking. He waits.
- **Quietly observant** — notices what is happening with the grandchildren before anyone else does. A slight smile signals he's noticed something important.
- **Wisdom through stillness** — he doesn't insert himself. He is available. When a grandchild looks to him, he offers just enough — never the full answer.
- **Warm with grandchildren** — a particular softness appears that isn't present elsewhere. He tolerates Zayan climbing on him. He listens to Sara fully.
- **Occasionally surprising** — he says something that lands unexpectedly. One sentence that reframes everything. Then he goes back to his tea.

---

## Clothing

| Scene type | Clothing |
|---|---|
| Home / morning | White or off-white cotton panjabi + cotton lungi. Simple, clean, worn comfortably. |
| Sitting with grandchildren | Same — perhaps with a thin shawl across shoulders in cooler light |
| More formal moments | White panjabi + white kufi |
| NEVER | Western clothing, formal suits, anything modern or branded |

---

## Environments

| Scene type | What Rahim is typically doing |
|---|---|
| Morning | Sitting on a low chair or floor mat, having tea. Watching light change through the window. |
| With grandchildren | Sitting nearby while they play or work. Occasionally leaning forward to observe more closely. |
| Evening | Prayer or sitting quietly. Sometimes with a worn Quran or newspaper. |
| Outdoors | In the courtyard, very still — a tree in the family's landscape |

---

## Key Expressions to Use in Veo3 Prompts

| Moment | What his face/body does |
|---|---|
| Watching a grandchild concentrate | Slightly forward lean, glasses on nose, quiet half-smile |
| A grandchild succeeds | Nods slowly once. Small smile. Does not clap or make a production of it. |
| Offering a hint | Leans slightly, speaks once — then sits back. Does not hover. |
| Watching Zayan's energy | Unhurried amusement. No anxiety in his body. |
| Sitting in background | Still, present, a warm weight in the room |

---

## Scene Assignment Logic

Use Rahim in:
- Storytelling videos (Pieces 46–48) — he is the elder anchor of the family narrative
- Frustration tolerance scenes (Piece 4) — Rahim watching Zayan fail and try again without interfering
- Patience and resilience scenes (Pieces 21, 26) — his stillness embodies what's being described
- Boredom scenes (Piece 6) — he sits near a bored Zayan, doing nothing to fix it. Just present.
- Any intergenerational moment — the bridge between how children were raised and how they're raised now

Do NOT use Rahim in fast, energetic reels. He belongs in slower, textured scenes.

---

## Veo3 Prompt Notes

When writing Rahim into a Veo3 prompt:
- Reference: "an elderly Bangladeshi man in his mid-60s in a simple white cotton panjabi and lungi, deep warm brown skin, grey-white short hair, gentle eyes behind simple metal-framed glasses, a slight natural stoop of age, patient and unhurried expression"
- He is almost always seated or very slowly moving
- His hands: "holding a tea glass," "resting on knees," "adjusting glasses," "turning something over slowly"
- Camera: wide or medium shot — let him exist in the scene, not fill it
