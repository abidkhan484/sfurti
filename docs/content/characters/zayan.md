# Zayan — Character Sheet
*Araf and Sonia's son. Younger sibling of Sara. Grandson of Rahim and Fatema.*

---

## Identity

| Property | Detail |
|---|---|
| Name | Zayan |
| Meaning | "Beautiful, adornment" (Arabic) |
| Role | The younger child — energetic, curious, emotionally transparent |
| Gender | Male |
| Age | 7 years old |
| Nationality | Bangladeshi |
| Relationship | Son of Araf and Sonia, younger brother of Sara (10), grandson of Rahim and Fatema |

---

## Physical Appearance

| Property | Detail |
|---|---|
| Skin tone | Warm brown, the healthy brown of a child who plays outdoors |
| Hair | Short black hair, slightly messy — not unkempt, just active. The kind that a mother smooths down and it springs back up in five minutes. |
| Face | Round, chubby cheeks starting to thin with age. Large dark brown eyes, wide and expressive. A gap in his front teeth (one adult tooth growing in). |
| Build | Small but dense with energy — compact, always in motion |
| Hands | Small, quick, sometimes still-sticky. He grabs things before he fully knows what he's going to do with them. |

---

## Personality (show through body language, never through text)

- **Energetic and easily distracted** — a dragonfly, attention moving fast
- **Deeply focused when something catches him** — when Zayan is hooked, he is hooked completely. He leans in, tongue slightly out, oblivious to everything else.
- **Emotionally transparent** — frustration shows immediately (a pout, pushing something away, looking at ceiling). Delight is even bigger — jumps, claps, looks around for someone to share it with.
- **Tries again** — after frustration, with a moment's reset, he goes back. He doesn't give up; he gives himself a dramatic break first.
- **Competitive with himself** — he wants to be the one who figured it out. Being helped too soon is deflating.

## Key Expressions to Use in Veo3 Prompts

| Moment | What his face/body does |
|---|---|
| Concentrating | Leans forward, tongue slightly out, one hand pressing on the surface in front of him |
| Just figured it out | Eyes go wide, then a full-face grin — he looks up immediately to find someone to show |
| Frustrated | Bottom lip pushed out, arms crossed briefly, looks at ceiling or pushes the object gently away |
| Trying again | Takes a breath, shifts position, leans back in — more focused this time |
| Being watched by Araf | Doesn't notice. He's elsewhere completely. |

---

## Scene Assignment Logic

Use Zayan in:
- Any scene involving a young child's hands working on something physical
- Frustration-tolerance scenes (Pieces 4, 21) — his emotional transparency makes these real
- "Breakthrough" or "aha moment" scenes — he sells delight better than anyone
- Scenes with Araf where the father watches without intervening
- Grandfather Rahim scenes — the two have a natural warmth (elder patience + child energy)
- Dopamine/focus/attention scenes where the contrast between screen-distracted and physically-absorbed is shown

Do NOT use Zayan as a narrator or for complex verbal explanation scenes.

**Camera note:** When filming Zayan, avoid showing his face clearly and identifiably — angle, blur background, or frame on hands/torso. Protect child identity in all Veo3 prompts.

---

## Veo3 Prompt Notes

When writing Zayan into a Veo3 prompt:
- Reference: "a 7-year-old Bangladeshi boy in a plain cotton t-shirt, warm brown skin, short slightly-messy black hair, large expressive dark eyes, small gap-toothed smile"
- Always specify what his hands are doing, not just his presence
- Frame: hands + partial face, or full body from distance — not straight close-up portrait
- His energy should read as real even in a still frame — use verbs: "leaning forward," "reaching," "pressing," "examining," "tongue slightly out in concentration"
