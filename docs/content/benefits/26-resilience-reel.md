## Veo3 Production

> A reel is **1 Veo3 scene (10s) + 5-second text outro** added in your editor.

### Scene 1 — Main Hook (10 seconds)

**Veo3 Prompt:**
Medium close-up shot, handheld documentary style. Araf, a Bangladeshi man in his early 30s in a simple white panjabi, warm brown skin, clean-shaven, innocent warm expression, stands front-facing in a warmly lit courtyard with soft afternoon light. He delivers his line with a warm, knowing smile, talking directly to the camera. In the background, Zayan (a 7-year-old Bangladeshi boy in a plain cotton t-shirt) is seen pushing something away in frustration, then after a beat, coming back to it. Rahim (an elderly Bangladeshi man in his mid-60s in a simple white cotton panjabi and lungi, deep warm brown skin, seated, patient and unhurried) is watching, completely still and unbothered.

**On-Screen Bangla Text Overlay:**
হার মানতে শেখা কেন জরুরি?

**Voiceover (Bangla — spoken over scene):**
জীবনের প্রথম হারটা যখন খেলার ছলে হয়, তখন বাচ্চা ব্যর্থতা মেনে নিতে শেখে। আর যে হার মেনে নিয়ে আবার চেষ্টা করতে পারে, জীবনে সে-ই এগিয়ে যায়।

## Notes for Creator
Araf should have an aura of wisdom but delivered gently, almost casually. The idea is to relieve parents of the pressure that their child always has to "win".
