## Veo3 Production

> A reel is **1 Veo3 scene (10s) + 5-second text outro** added in your editor.

### Scene 1 — Main Hook (10 seconds)

**Veo3 Prompt:**
Medium close-up shot, handheld documentary style. Araf, a Bangladeshi man in his early 30s in a simple white panjabi, warm brown skin, clean-shaven, innocent warm expression, stands in a warm, natural-lit room. He looks directly at the camera with an honest, warm expression and gestures naturally with his hands as if asking a genuine question. In the softly blurred background, Rahim (an elderly Bangladeshi man in his mid-60s in a simple white cotton panjabi and lungi, deep warm brown skin, grey-white hair, gentle eyes behind simple metal-framed glasses, seated, patient and unhurried — holding a tea glass and watching quietly) sits perfectly still. Cozy Bangladeshi living room with wooden furniture and amber window light. 

**On-Screen Bangla Text Overlay:**
ধৈর্য কি জন্মগত?

**Voiceover (Bangla — spoken over scene):**
ধৈর্য কোনো জন্মগত স্বভাব নয়, এটা একটা স্কিল। বাচ্চারা যখন বারবার চেষ্টা করে কোনো সমস্যার সমাধান করে, তখন তারা ধৈর্য ধরতে শেখে।
