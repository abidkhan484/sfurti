## Veo3 Production

> A reel is **1 Veo3 scene (10s) + 5-second text outro** added in your editor.

### Scene 1 — Main Hook (10 seconds)

**Veo3 Prompt:**
Close-up shot, handheld documentary style at floor level. Zayan, a 7-year-old Bangladeshi boy in a plain cotton t-shirt, warm brown skin, short slightly-messy black hair, large expressive eyes, leaning forward in concentration to solve something alone. Framed on hands and torso, not direct face close-up, then his expression of success is visible from the side. In the background, Araf (a Bangladeshi man in his early 30s in a simple white panjabi, warm brown skin, clean-shaven, innocent warm expression, watching quietly) is seen stepping back, deliberately NOT helping. Earthy, warm tones, natural and honest aesthetic.

**On-Screen Bangla Text Overlay:**
আসল আত্মবিশ্বাস কীভাবে তৈরি হয়?

**Voiceover (Bangla — spoken over scene):**
শুধু প্রশংসা করলেই আত্মবিশ্বাস বাড়ে না। বাচ্চা যখন নিজে নিজে কোনো একটা সমাধান বের করে, তখন ভেতর থেকে যে জোরটা তৈরি হয়, সেটাই আসল আত্মবিশ্বাস।
