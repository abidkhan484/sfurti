## Nano Banana Image Prompt

### How to use this prompt:
1. Open Nano Banana (app.nanabanana.ai or your interface)
2. Select "Text to Image" or "Generate Image"
3. Set dimensions: **1080×1350px** (portrait, more reach)
4. Paste the prompt below exactly
5. If result feels off, regenerate 2–3 times before modifying the prompt
6. Download highest resolution available

### Prompt:
```
A photorealistic warm-toned lifestyle photograph of a young Bangladeshi child (around 5 years old) sitting on a warm wooden floor, deeply absorbed in building something with natural wooden blocks. The child has a look of pure concentration and joy on their face. The camera angle is slightly above eye level. The composition uses the rule of thirds, leaving the entire left side or top third empty for text. The lighting is soft natural golden hour window light, casting gentle warm shadows. The color palette is earthy and warm, featuring ochre, warm white, and natural wood tones. Candid documentary style, natural imperfection, authentic Bangladeshi home setting, photorealistic, high detail.
```

### Style Modifier (add to prompt if tone feels wrong):
- Too cold/corporate: add "warm golden hour window light, soft shadows, intimate home setting"
- Too staged: add "candid documentary style, natural imperfection, authentic Bangladeshi home"
- Needs more texture: add "slight film grain, organic textures, handmade feel"
