## Facebook

### Full Facebook Post
```
I used to think helping was the job.

Quick to jump in. Quick to guide her hand to the right piece. Quick to end the frustration before it got too big.

Last Sunday I tried something different. I put my coffee down, stood in the doorway, and I just watched.

She had a 12-piece wooden puzzle in front of her. She tried. Failed. Tried. Got frustrated. Pushed it away. Looked at me.

I smiled and said nothing.

She looked back at the puzzle. Picked up the piece. Turned it. Once. Twice. And then — it clicked.

And then they all clicked.

She looked up at me when it was done and her face — that face — is the reason I do this.

Not because I helped.

Because I didn't.

The best thing I gave her that morning wasn't a hand. It was 12 minutes of space to figure it out herself.

That's what we're here for. 🧩
```
