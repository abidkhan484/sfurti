# The First Puzzle — Production Script

**Story Idea:** A young child encounters a wooden puzzle for the first time. After failing, getting frustrated, and almost giving up — she solves it. Her mother watches the whole time without saying a word.

**Format:** Short-form video  
**Duration:** 60 seconds  
**Tone:** Emotional / Inspirational  
**Platform:** TikTok, Instagram Reels, Facebook  
**Style:** Cinematic live-action  
**Language:** English  
**Audience:** Parents of children ages 3–8, Bangladesh  
**Character:** A 5-year-old girl and her mother  

---

## Scene 1 — THE TABLE
**Duration:** 6 seconds  
**Setting:** A warm, sunlit kitchen table. Morning. Soft golden light streams through a gauze curtain. A single wooden puzzle sits on the table — bright colours, 12 pieces.  
**Shot:** WIDE — table level, child not yet visible  
**Action:** The puzzle sits alone on the table. A small hand enters the frame and picks up a piece, examines it, turns it slowly.

**Narration:**  
> *(no narration — silence)*

**Voiceover notes:** Silent. Let the ambient sound breathe — soft wood-on-table sounds, distant birds.

**Music / Sound:** Soft room tone only. Single distant bird. A light kitchen hum. No music yet.  
**OpenMontage pipeline:** `cinematic-trailer`

---

## Scene 2 — THE ATTEMPT
**Duration:** 8 seconds  
**Setting:** Same table. Camera moves to MEDIUM on the child's face — full concentration.  
**Shot:** MEDIUM — child's hands and face  
**Action:** She tries to fit a piece. It doesn't go. She tries again. Wrong. She turns it around three times, frowning slightly. Puts it down. Picks up another.

**Narration:**  
> *(no narration — only sound)*

**Voiceover notes:** Silent. The effort speaks for itself.

**Music / Sound:** First note of a single piano key. Soft. Like a breath. Holds.  
**OpenMontage pipeline:** `cinematic-trailer`

---

## Scene 3 — THE FRUSTRATION
**Duration:** 7 seconds  
**Setting:** Same. Slightly tighter on her face.  
**Shot:** CLOSE-UP — her brow furrowing, lip slightly bitten  
**Action:** She pushes a piece hard — it's still wrong. She pulls back her hands. Looks away. Her jaw tightens. She is about to stop.

**Narration:**  
> *(no narration)*

**Voiceover notes:** Still silent. The music swells very slightly — one additional piano note.

**Music / Sound:** Two piano notes now — minor, but not sad. Thoughtful.  
**OpenMontage pipeline:** `cinematic-trailer`

---

## Scene 4 — THE MOTHER
**Duration:** 5 seconds  
**Setting:** Same room. Camera shifts to the doorway — the mother stands there watching. She does not step forward. She does not speak.  
**Shot:** MEDIUM — mother's face in the doorway, soft focus background  
**Action:** The mother watches. Her expression is not anxious. It is patient. Knowing. A slight half-smile at the corner of her mouth.

**Narration:**  
> *(no narration)*

**Voiceover notes:** Still silent. The mother's stillness IS the message.

**Music / Sound:** Piano softens further — almost inaudible. Room tone.  
**OpenMontage pipeline:** `cinematic-trailer`

---

## Scene 5 — THE RETURN
**Duration:** 8 seconds  
**Setting:** Back on the child. Her hands return to the puzzle.  
**Shot:** MEDIUM then slowly zooming to CLOSE-UP on hands  
**Action:** She picks up the original piece she dropped. Turns it slowly — once, twice. Something shifts in her face. She lowers it to the board. It fits. Her hands freeze.

**Narration:**  
> *(no narration)*

**Voiceover notes:** First hint of warm music — a single string note joins the piano.

**Music / Sound:** Piano + cello note. A held chord. Tentative.  
**OpenMontage pipeline:** `cinematic-trailer`

---

## Scene 6 — THE SOLVE
**Duration:** 6 seconds  
**Setting:** Same table. Close on the puzzle board.  
**Shot:** EXTREME CLOSE-UP → PULL BACK to reveal the completed puzzle  
**Action:** One by one — fast now — she places the remaining pieces. Click. Click. Click. The final piece drops in. She sits back. The puzzle is complete.

**Narration:**  
> *(no narration)*

**Voiceover notes:** The music builds here — warm, rising. Not triumphant. Earned.

**Music / Sound:** Piano + strings building — warm, slow rise. Joy without drama.  
**OpenMontage pipeline:** `cinematic-trailer`

---

## Scene 7 — THE LOOK
**Duration:** 5 seconds  
**Setting:** She turns to find her mother. The mother is still there.  
**Shot:** TWO-SHOT — child and mother in the same frame for the first time  
**Action:** The child looks up at her mother. The mother nods — one slow, proud nod. No words. The child turns back to the puzzle, satisfied.

**Narration:**  
> *"Some things can't be taught."*

**Voiceover notes:** Warm, slow, almost a whisper. Pause before the line. Let it land.

**Music / Sound:** Full piano + strings at peak. One held warm chord.  
**OpenMontage pipeline:** `cinematic-trailer`

---

## Scene 8 — THE CLOSE
**Duration:** 6 seconds  
**Setting:** The puzzle, complete. Table level shot. Then fade.  
**Shot:** WIDE → slow fade to white  
**Action:** The completed puzzle sits on the table. Light catches the wood grain. Slow fade.

**Narration:**  
> *"They just have to be given the chance to find it."*

**Voiceover notes:** Quieter than scene 7. Let the sentence breathe. No rush.

**Music / Sound:** Music fades to single piano note. Silence. End.
